## 🧪 Research Focus

Justice’s current PhD research centers on intelligent transportation and robust machine learning, with an emphasis on:

### **• V2X Communication**  
Designing systems that enable vehicles, infrastructure, and edge devices to exchange information efficiently and reliably.

### **• Reinforcement Learning (RL)**  
Developing RL-based decision-making agents, with attention to stability, robustness, and real-world feasibility.

### **• SUMO Traffic Simulation**  
Using the SUMO microscopic traffic simulator to evaluate ML-driven traffic control strategies in realistic, large-scale urban settings.

### **• Digital Twins for Intelligent Transportation**  
Creating virtual replicas of road networks and communication systems to safely test algorithms before deployment—  
an approach increasingly used by leading AI and automotive research labs.
